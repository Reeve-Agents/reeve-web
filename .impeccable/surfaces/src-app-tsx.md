# Studio (owner-facing)

Scope: the studio route (`?view=studio`), phone first, iPad and desktop as a centred column. Mode: Operate.
Audience and job: a UMKM owner, not technical, sets up how their business runs, approves it with a fingerprint, and comes back to change a rule or see what ran.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Setting up Reeve feels like setting up a new iPhone feature: a home checklist of six steps, one task per screen, the next action always at the thumb. It refuses the developer dashboard: no boxed panels, no logs, hashes or model names in view.

OWN-WORLD: The landing's night palette on iOS structure. Void black ground; inset grouped lists filled in ink #001F3F with inset hairline separators, never outlined boxes; lime #C6FF33 is the one tint (primary buttons, done marks); violet marks "never" rules, trust blue marks targets. Inter Tight carries all UI on an iOS text scale; one Instrument Serif italic phrase in the home large title is the owner's voice.

STORY: Home shows where the business stands ("4 dari 6 langkah"), one highlighted row. Ceritakan is a chat with Reeve. Aturan shows each rule as a Shortcuts-style block (KALAU → LAKUKAN). Uji coba says "Lulus 29 dari 29 pemeriksaan" in words. Setujui is a Face ID sheet. Jalan and Bukti read like Wallet activity.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Large title with the business name, progress line, the six-step inset list with the current row lit, a docked lime button for that step.

SIGNATURE: The rule block: SOP sentence becomes a tinted KALAU/LAKUKAN block, the same object in chat, rules, test results and edits.

RISK: Hiding technical proof can look like hiding it; every screen keeps a quiet "Lihat detail" sheet with the real numbers.
